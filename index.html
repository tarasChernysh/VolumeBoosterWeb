Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about making your iPhone louder with EQfy.

How do I make my iPhone louder than the maximum volume?

Open a song or audio file in EQfy and turn the Volume dial past 100%. EQfy adds up to +12 dB of extra gain (200%) on top of your iPhone’s volume — no jailbreak or extra hardware. It works for audio played inside EQfy. Read the full guide.

Is EQfy free to use?

Yes, EQfy is free to download. Volume Boost, music discovery, the offline player and importing from your Music library and Files are free. Premium ($1.99/week or $27.99/year in the US; prices vary by country) unlocks Bass Boost, the Equalizer, 3D Audio, balance, speed and haptics. The AI tools (songs, sound effects, speech and the Noise Reducer) use credits.

Will boosting the volume damage my hearing or speakers?

Loud sound for long periods can harm your hearing, and extreme boosts can make small speakers distort. Raise the dial gradually, back off if you hear crackling, and keep headphone listening at moderate levels. How to boost volume safely.

Does EQfy work with Spotify, YouTube or phone calls?

No. iOS only lets apps process the audio they play themselves, so EQfy boosts the songs and files you play inside EQfy — through the iPhone speaker, wired or Bluetooth headphones and speakers. It can’t change the volume of other apps, calls or system sounds.

Can I use it with Apple Music and offline?

You can import songs from your iPhone’s Music library and from the Files app and play them offline. Songs protected by an Apple Music subscription play in EQfy, but iOS doesn’t allow the boost and EQ tools on them — purchased or unprotected tracks and your own files work with every tool.

What do the Bass Booster and Equalizer do?

Bass Boost adds up to +12 dB of low end, tuned so iPhone speakers can reproduce it. The Equalizer has 7 bands from 32 Hz to 16 kHz plus a preamp, with 9 presets and a Custom curve. See the best EQ settings by genre.

Which devices does EQfy support?

EQfy is built for iPhone and needs iOS 16 or later. It’s available worldwide on the App Store in 25 languages. iPhone Duo support, with the full inner display and a side-by-side layout, has been available since v2.2.23. Read more.

Is EQfy the same app as Volume Booster Sound Amplifier?

Yes. EQfy - Louder Volume is listed on the US App Store as “Volume Booster Sound Amplifier” (App ID 6550912161). The name can differ by country, but it’s the same app from the same developer.
