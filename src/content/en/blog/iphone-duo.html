iPhone Duo support is here in EQfy - Louder Volume v2.2.23. On the big inner display, the app now uses the full screen instead of a letter-boxed phone layout, with sidebar navigation and two-column screens. That puts EQfy among the iPhone Duo apps that make real use of the extra space. The same update also gives every iPhone a side-by-side player in landscape.

Available now: v2.2.23 was released on the App Store on October 14, 2026. Everything below describes what it changed.

Why iPhone Duo needs its own layout

iPhone Duo is Apple's first foldable iPhone. According to Apple's iPhone Duo page, it opens to a 7.6-inch inner display and closes to a 5.4-inch outer display. An app designed only for phone-sized screens leaves much of that inner display unused. Version 2.2.23 makes EQfy feel at home on both screens, so the volume booster on iPhone Duo is as easy to use open as it is closed.

What changed on the inner display

When you unfold iPhone Duo, EQfy fills the whole inner display and rearranges its screens to suit the width.

Sidebar navigation

On iOS 27, EQfy's four sections (Home, Music, AI Studio and Settings) move into a sidebar on the inner display. You switch sections from the side of the screen instead of the bottom. When the window is narrow, the familiar tab bar stays.

Home in two columns

The tool cards and your Recent list sit side by side. You can start a boost or reopen a recent track from the same view, without scrolling between them.

A two-column player

Open any track and the active tool sits in one column, whether it's the Volume or Bass dial, the EQ sliders, the Headphones balance or 3D Audio. The track info and playback controls sit in the other. You see the dial and the controls at the same time.

A wider AI Studio

The AI Studio grid grows from two columns to four on wide displays, so Music Generator, Sound Effects, Text to Speech and Noise Reducer fit in a single row.

Readable forms and sheets

Settings, the AI forms and the promo-code screen keep a comfortable reading width instead of stretching edge to edge. The subscription screen opens as a page sheet on wide layouts.

Landscape got better on every iPhone

The two-column player isn't only for iPhone Duo. In v2.2.23 it also appears whenever you turn any iPhone to landscape. That fixes a real annoyance: in landscape, parts of the player's controls used to be clipped. Now the tool and the playback controls sit next to each other and stay fully visible.

On the outer display

Closed, iPhone Duo works like a compact iPhone, and EQfy keeps its familiar phone layouts there, with a compact floating tab bar on the right edge. All of the screenshots below were taken on the outer display.

Also new in v2.2.23

  • A short welcome quiz that builds a personal sound profile based on how you listen.
  • Earlier trial reminders: free-trial reminders now arrive before your trial ends.
  • A fix for a blank screen when adding songs from your Music library on Home.

Same sound tools, more room

The layout is new; the sound tools stay the same. On iPhone Duo you get everything EQfy offers on any iPhone:

  • Volume Boost: up to 200%, or +12 dB on top of your iPhone volume. Free.
  • Bass Boost: up to +12 dB, tuned so small speakers can reproduce it.
  • 7-band equalizer: 32 Hz to 16 kHz plus a preamp, with 9 presets.
  • Headphone balance: shift sound left or right.
  • 3D Audio: reverb rooms and sound that moves around you.
  • AI song generator and the rest of AI Studio.

Volume Boost and the music player are free. The other player tools are part of Premium.

Good to know: EQfy boosts and shapes audio played inside EQfy only. iOS doesn't let it process Spotify, YouTube, Apple Music streaming, phone or FaceTime calls, and that doesn't change on iPhone Duo.

How to get it

v2.2.23 is a normal App Store update, so if you already have EQfy, update it to get the new layouts. If you don't have it yet, EQfy is free to download and needs iOS 16 or later. Find it on the App Store (in the US it's listed as Volume Booster Sound Amplifier), or open the App Store page. Questions? The EQfy FAQ covers pricing, devices and more.
