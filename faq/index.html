EQfy FAQ: Volume Booster & Equalizer Help

Quick answers about EQfy - Louder Volume, the iPhone app for louder sound, deeper bass and better-sounding music. Can't find your question? Email support@eqfy.app.

Getting started

What is EQfy - Louder Volume?

EQfy - Louder Volume is an iPhone app that makes the music you play in it louder and fuller. It combines Volume Boost up to 200%, Bass Boost, a 7-band equalizer, headphone balance, 3D Audio and an AI Studio with a free music library and player that also works offline. See all features.

Is EQfy free?

Yes. EQfy is free to download, and Volume Boost and the music library and player are free to use. Bass Boost, the equalizer, headphone balance, 3D Audio, playback speed and the haptic player are part of the optional Premium subscription.

Is EQfy the same app as Volume Booster Sound Amplifier on the App Store?

Yes. On the US App Store, EQfy - Louder Volume is listed as Volume Booster Sound Amplifier, and the listing name can differ in other countries. It's the same app, made by developer Taras Chernysh: open it on the App Store. Apps with similar names come from other developers; EQfy's App Store ID is 6550912161.

Which iPhones and languages does EQfy support?

EQfy is built for iPhone and requires iOS 16 or later. The app is available in 25 languages.

Volume & bass

How much louder can EQfy make my iPhone?

Volume Boost goes from 100% to 200%, which adds up to +12 dB of gain on top of your iPhone volume for songs played in EQfy. Use it moderately. Learn more in how to make your iPhone louder than max volume and is volume boosting safe.

Does the boost work with the iPhone speaker and headphones?

Yes. EQfy's tools apply to everything EQfy plays, whether you listen through the iPhone speaker, wired headphones or AirPods. For more tips, see how to make AirPods louder.

Why can't EQfy boost Spotify, YouTube or calls?

iOS sandboxing only lets EQfy process audio played inside EQfy. It can't boost Spotify, YouTube, Apple Music streaming, Netflix, phone or FaceTime calls, notifications or the system volume. For fixes that help everywhere, see iPhone volume too low.

Can I boost songs from Apple Music?

Songs from an Apple Music subscription, and other protected files, play in EQfy, but the enhancement tools aren't available for them because iOS doesn't allow apps to process protected audio. Purchased or unprotected songs from your Music library and files you import from Files work with every tool.

How does Bass Boost work?

Bass Boost adds up to +12 dB with a low-shelf boost centered around 200 Hz, tuned so small iPhone speakers can actually reproduce it. It's part of Premium. See how to boost bass on iPhone.

Sound tools

What does EQfy's equalizer include?

Seven bands (32 Hz, 64 Hz, 200 Hz, 500 Hz, 1 kHz, 4 kHz and 16 kHz) plus a preamp, 9 presets (Rock, Jazz, Electronic, Classical, Reggae, Rap, R&B, Dance and Acoustic), a Custom setting and a Reset button. See the best EQ settings for iPhone by genre.

What is 3D Audio in EQfy?

It's an in-app effect with two modes: Reverb, with 9 room, hall and chamber presets, and 3D Source, which moves the sound around you with 4 Auto Motion patterns (bilateral, orbit, figure-eight and breathing) and can use head tracking with supported AirPods. It isn't Apple Spatial Audio or Dolby Atmos. 3D audio on iPhone, explained covers the difference.

Can I fix one earbud sounding louder than the other?

For songs played in EQfy, the Headphones tab has a left/right audio balance slider with a Centre button to reset it. See how to fix left/right headphone balance on iPhone.

Can I change playback speed or export enhanced songs?

Yes. Playback speed (0.5× to 2×) and the haptic player, which makes your iPhone vibrate to the beat, are part of Premium. You can save and share the enhanced audio file with the share button in the player; it includes your speed, volume boost, equalizer and bass settings, but not reverb, 3D Audio or balance.

AI Studio

What's in AI Studio?

AI Studio has four tools: Music Generator (compose a song from a text prompt, with an optional Force instrumental switch), Sound Effects, Text to Speech and Noise Reducer. All of them need an internet connection. Read the AI song generator guide.

How do AI credits work?

AI Studio is credit-based, and every tool uses credits: Music Generator costs 3,000 credits, Sound Effects 250, Text to Speech 500 and Noise Reducer 1,500 per started minute. You can buy credit packs in the app or earn credits in the Credit Shop. Credits are deducted only after a result is saved.

Pricing & subscription

What does Premium include?

Premium unlocks Bass Boost, the 7-band equalizer, headphone balance, 3D Audio, playback speed and the haptic player. Volume Boost and the music player stay free for everyone, and the AI Studio tools use credits.

How much does Premium cost, and is there a free trial?

Premium is a weekly or yearly subscription. In the US it costs $1.99 per week or $27.99 per year; prices vary by country, and the App Store shows your local price before you confirm. Eligible plans start with a free trial.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, choose the EQfy subscription and tap Cancel Subscription. Apple explains the steps in Cancel a subscription from Apple.

Devices

Does EQfy support iPhone Duo?

Yes. EQfy has supported iPhone Duo since v2.2.23, released on October 14, 2026: it uses the full inner display with sidebar navigation and two-column layouts, and every iPhone gets a side-by-side player in landscape. See what's new.

Does EQfy work on iPad?

EQfy is built for iPhone. You can install it on an iPad, where it runs in iPhone mode, but it isn't designed as an iPad app.

Privacy & support

What data does EQfy collect?

EQfy doesn't require an account. It uses analytics and advertising services, asks for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency before using your advertising identifier, and sends AI Studio requests to a third-party AI provider for processing. The Privacy Policy has the full details.

How do I contact support?

Email support@eqfy.app. Tell us your iPhone model and iOS version and describe what happened, so we can help faster.
