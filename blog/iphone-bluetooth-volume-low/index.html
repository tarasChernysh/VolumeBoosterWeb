iPhone Bluetooth Volume Too Low? Fixes That Work

By the EQfy team · Published October 21, 2026 · 3 min read

If your iPhone's Bluetooth volume is too low, check both volume controls first: the iPhone's and the accessory's own. Then set Device Type for the device in Settings › Bluetooth, forget the device and pair it again, move away from interference, and restart the iPhone. For headphones, also check Reduce Loud Audio. Apple documents each of these steps except the two-volume tip, which is general advice.

This guide is about sound that is quiet when it goes through Bluetooth, such as a speaker, headphones or a car stereo. If the iPhone is quiet everywhere, start with iPhone Volume Too Low? 12 Quick Fixes to Try.

1. Turn up both volume controls

Many Bluetooth speakers, headphones and car stereos have their own volume level in addition to the iPhone's. The sound you hear is limited by whichever one is lower. So raise the volume on the iPhone with the buttons or the Control Center slider, and also on the device itself, using its buttons, knob or companion app. Apple's page If your headphones don't work with your iPhone or iPad covers the iPhone side.

If you listen on a car stereo, check its volume, the input source and any input-level or sound settings in the car's manual. Apple's CarPlay help covers connection problems, not loudness.

2. Set the Device Type

For third-party devices, Apple's iPhone User Guide says you can improve the sound by telling the iPhone what kind of Bluetooth device it is.

  1. Go to Settings › Bluetooth.
  2. Tap the info icon next to the device.
  3. Tap Device Type and choose headphone, speaker or car stereo.

3. Forget the device and pair it again

A stale pairing can make the sound thin or quiet. Per Apple's guide:

  1. Go to Settings › Bluetooth.
  2. Tap the info icon next to the accessory, then tap Forget This Device and confirm.
  3. Put the accessory in pairing mode and connect it again.

Apple's Bluetooth troubleshooting page also suggests turning the accessory off and on, and making sure it's charged. If you forget a pair of AirPods, they're removed from your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account, so you'll need to pair them again there too.

4. Cut interference and get closer

Apple's guide says accessories pair within about 10 meters (33 feet), and walls, floors and crowded Wi-Fi areas can weaken the connection. Apple's page on distorted or dropping Bluetooth audio suggests keeping the iPhone and accessory close, with nothing solid in between, and away from sources of interference such as microwaves. Apple's guide also says that turning on Airplane Mode may eliminate audio interference between the iPhone and an accessory, and that moving or turning the iPhone and the accessory can improve wireless performance.

5. For headphones: check Reduce Loud Audio

If it's only quiet in headphones, open Settings › Sounds & Haptics › Headphone Safety and check Reduce Loud Audio. When it's on with a low limit, it caps the level. Apple's guide to headphone audio levels explains the slider. For AirPods, see how to make AirPods louder.

6. Restart and update

Restart the iPhone, and update to the latest iOS, which Apple recommends on that same page. If the problem happens with only one accessory, look in the maker's app or manual for a firmware update, because Apple doesn't document updating third-party accessories.

7. If only some songs are quiet

If most audio is fine and a few songs are quiet, the file itself may be the problem. Songs you play in EQfy - Louder Volume can be boosted by up to +12 dB with the free Volume Boost dial, which also helps when the Bluetooth device has a low maximum. Import the song from your Music library or the Files app, and see our guide to making your iPhone louder than max volume for the settings to check first.

What EQfy can't do: iOS only lets EQfy process audio played inside the app. It can't change a Bluetooth device's own volume, or the volume of Spotify, YouTube, calls and other apps. See how Volume Boost works.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my iPhone Bluetooth volume so low?

Usually one of the volume controls is low: the iPhone's, the accessory's own, or a limit such as Reduce Loud Audio for headphones. A weak connection, a stale pairing or a wrong Device Type setting can also make the sound thin or quiet.

How do I re-pair a Bluetooth device on iPhone?

Go to Settings, tap Bluetooth, tap the info icon next to the device, tap Forget This Device and confirm. Then put the accessory in pairing mode and connect it again, as described on Apple's support page.

What is the Device Type setting for Bluetooth?

For third-party Bluetooth devices, Apple says you can improve the sound by telling the iPhone what the device is. Open Settings, tap Bluetooth, tap the info icon next to the device, tap Device Type and choose headphone, speaker or car stereo.

Can an app make my Bluetooth speaker louder?

Only for audio that the app plays itself. EQfy can boost a song you play in it by up to +12 dB, but it can't change the speaker's own volume, Spotify, YouTube or phone calls.
